One distinction to keep in the design: for a single uninterrupted task, finish-by minus duration gives the latest feasible start imposed by that finish deadline. An independently chosen latest-start can be earlier and express a different need. With both present, the effective latest start is the earlier of the explicit latest-start and finish-by minus duration. So the separate field can be useful even without splitting.

Let's use “start no earlier than” and “start no later than” consistently; calling the earliest bound “start-by” is already making this harder to follow.
