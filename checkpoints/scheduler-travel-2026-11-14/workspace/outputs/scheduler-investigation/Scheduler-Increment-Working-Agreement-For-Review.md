## Definition of done for an increment
- It demonstrates its stated user-relevant behavior at the disclosed proof layer.
- Exact proposed/applied diffs, input/runtime pins, invocation, bounds and expected observations are reviewable.
- Independent review is reconciled before launch review.
- If execution is authorized separately, actual retained evidence supports the claim and owned resources are retired/cleaned under their exact authority.
- The plan records completed behavior, limitations and the next increment. No global goal closure from a smaller demonstration.

Existing evidence is reusable when it proves the asserted property and relevant source/dependency lineage is verified. Pure-validator/fault-control evidence does not automatically need real TLS repetition. Actual socket/TLS/timing/thread and database-acknowledgment claims still require evidence at those layers. This is evidence matching, not a waiver.
