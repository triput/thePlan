Make the **scheduler** treat **converted / assigned new rules** as scheduling-effective under the approved **May / Must / Prefer** composition model: **May** alternatives **union**; **Must** requirements **intersect**; **Prefer** **ranks only** among otherwise eligible candidates. **Prefer never creates availability** and never waives a requirement. Resolve **authorized scoped exceptions** from [§2.1](#21-exception-operation-matrix) into effective inputs **before** evaluating the eligibility stack (SPEC §4.2 / §4.4).

### 2.1 Exception operation matrix

**Filled v1.5.** This is the contract Dex §15 asked for. Product rules stay **V4-P1–P4** (§14) and SPEC §4.2 / §4.4. Kind labels below are **engineering stubs**, not locked routes or table names. A rejected row leaves saved configuration **unchanged** (no silent ignore, no partial apply). Allowed writes still take the owner lock and bump `scheduling_revision` (§2 full activation boundary). A rejected write does not.

**Narrower than full SPEC §4.4 — named deferral.** SPEC §4.4 scopes an exception to one task, a **date range**, or a **standing-rule change**, and includes **expiry** plus overlapping **dated** availability. V4 ships only **standing** operations (they persist until a later explicit change or removal) at **task / project / epic**. **Removal** of a standing named-rule row restores that named underlying rule. **Not in V4, and not “done” by omission:**

- Date-range exceptions, one-date permits, expiry clocks, recurring **weekday-standing** rules, and SPEC §4.4 dated-overlap resolution (“which replaces which”) → **S2-V4b**, with day-scoped replacement vs exclusive (**A14b**). The SPEC §4.4 “one specified Monday” example is this dated form.
- Context-scoped exceptions, including a waiver of a context or resource requirement → **S2-V7**.
- Generic **ignore-all-restrictions** → **reject** (SPEC §4.4). Not parked on a later slice.

**`time_map_overrides`** stays legacy civil-day replacement of map bands (V3). It is not a V4 exception store and does not gain a second meaning.

#### Whole-assignment family (V4-P1 / V4-P2)

These switch, remove, or restore the **local assignment as a whole**. They are not a named-rule add, replace, or waiver.

| Operation | Target (rule / assignment) | Supported scope (task / project / epic) | Persistence | Deferred forms → reject (no silent ignore) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Family replace** (explicit) | Whole local **assignment family** at that scope (legacy map ↔ new-rule family) | One of task, project, epic | Persists until clear, revert, or another explicit replace. Prior local assignment, including “no local assignment,” is kept for revert. | Date-range or weekday-only family replace → **reject, no write** (**V4b**). Context-conditioned replace → **reject, no write** (**V7**). Ordinary colliding write without explicit replace → **atomic reject, no write** (P1). Not OPT-CONVERT. |
| **Clear** | Whole **current local assignment** at that scope | One of task, project, epic | Removes the local assignment and re-resolves inheritance. Does **not** restore a displaced local map. Stays cleared until a later assignment write. Revision-checked. | Clear-for-a-date-range or clear-on-weekdays → **reject, no write** (**V4b**). Clear is not revert. |
| **Revert** | Prior local assignment captured by **one identified replacement** | Same scope as that replacement | Restores only that captured state. Does not rewind parent settings or shared policy hours. Revision-checked (expected revision / current assignment). | V4 has no dated replacement to undo; a date-scoped or weekday revert → **reject, no write** (**V4b**). Stale revision, or a removed/unusable prior map or policy → **reject with a repair choice**, no silent fallback. Not a history browser. Does not move scheduled blocks. |

#### Named-rule exception (V4-P4 / SPEC §4.4)

Standing only. Each row changes **one named rule** at the stated scope and is applied to effective inputs **before** May union, Must intersect, and Prefer rank (SPEC §4.2 step 1 / §4.4). Later layers do not invent permission. These rows do not clear busy time or unrelated requirements, and they do not switch assignment family.

Named-rule rows require the **new-rule family** already active at that scope. On an unconverted legacy assignment they **reject, no write** (P4: do not silently apply a new rule to a legacy assignment). Switching family is **family replace**, not a named-rule operation.

| Operation | Target (rule / assignment) | Supported scope (task / project / epic) | Persistence | Deferred forms → reject (no silent ignore) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Named-rule addition** | One new named **May**, **Must**, or **Prefer** on the local new-rule assignment. May addition is SPEC §4.4 “add a permitted window” and **unions** with inherited May; it does not erase an ancestor Must. Must addition **intersects**. Prefer addition **ranks only** and does not create hours or waive Must. | One of task, project, epic | Persists until a later explicit change or removal. Removal drops that named addition and restores the underlying rule set for it. **No expiry clock in V4.** **Before stack.** | Same addition limited to a date range, one calendar day, or a standing weekday → **reject, no write** (**V4b**). Context-scoped addition → **reject, no write** (**V7**). |
| **Named-rule replacement** | One **named** inherited rule (May, Must, or Prefer). Replaces that rule only; sibling rules stay. Explicit empty May or empty Must as the replacement **content** follows P3 (empty stays empty; no fall-through to General). | One of task, project, epic | Persists until a later explicit change or removal. Removal restores that named inherited rule. **Before stack.** | Day/date replacement, or an unresolved overlapping dated “which replaces which” → **reject, no write** (**V4b**; P4). |
| **Named-rule waiver** | One **named Must** | One of task, project, epic | Persists until removal. Removal restores that Must. **Before stack:** intersection omits the waived Must only. Other Musts, Prefer, busy, and context stay. | Waive-for-a-date-range or waive-on-weekdays → **reject, no write** (**V4b**). Waive a context or resource requirement → **reject, no write** (**V7**). Ignore-all-restrictions → **reject, no write** (forbidden; not deferred). |

**Permitted, before composition:** a task-scope **named-rule addition** of a May window is written onto that task and resolved into effective inputs **before** May union / Must intersect / Prefer rank. It does not permit other tasks, does not waive the parent Must (P4), and does not clear busy.

**Unsupported, reject with no changes:** the same task asked to be permitted on **one specified Monday**, on **every Monday**, or by waiving a **context** requirement. The write is rejected. Saved assignment and rules stay as they were. Monday and weekday-standing → **V4b**. Context → **V7**.

#### Not an exception store

| Operation | Target (rule / assignment) | Supported scope (task / project / epic) | Persistence | Deferred forms → reject (no silent ignore) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Legacy day override** | Existing `time_map_overrides` row on a focus window | Legacy map, one civil scheduling day (V3). Not a task/project/epic exception scope. | Unchanged: civil-day replacement of that map’s bands. | A request to store a May/Must/Prefer exception, named waiver, or family replace/clear/revert **as** a `time_map_overrides` row → **reject, no write**. No second meaning. |

**Engineering stubs (not product locks):** `assignment.family_replace`, `assignment.clear`, `assignment.revert`, `rule.add`, `rule.replace_named`, `rule.waive_named`. HTTP paths and storage names are an Implementation choice. Do not reuse `tasks.source_refs`, `preferred_time_window_id`, or `time_map_overrides` (§12). Omitted/inherit, clear-assignment, and explicit-empty-set stay **distinct representations** (P3); they are not extra exception kinds hiding outside this matrix.

## 5. Composition contract (restated from authority — not new product)

From SPEC §4.2 / Dex redesign review / S0 — already approved language; V4 implements it for **activated** new rules:

1. Resolve assignments, origins, dates, weekday scope, and **scoped exceptions** first.
2. Determine declared eligible availability (May union among alternatives).
3. Intersect Must requirements; subtract exclusions / busy / hard constraints.
4. Rank remaining candidates with Prefer (rank-only).
5. If none remain, explain — never silently relax a requirement.

V4 runs that order for the **standing** rows in [§2.1](#21-exception-operation-matrix) only. A date-range or weekday-standing exception request rejects before this stack (**V4b**); a context-scoped request rejects the same way (**V7**).

**V3 handoff:** exposed saved new-rule config stayed inactive; Working/General + legacy maps already scheduling-effective. V4 flips new-rule activation under this stack without rewriting V3 Working/General SoT.
